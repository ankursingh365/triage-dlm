HOW TO USE THIS
---------------
60 wrong answers, in random order. For each one, read the
trace and write ONE letter in the blank. About 30 minutes.

The machine's own label is deliberately NOT shown. If you see it first
you will agree with it, and the agreement number this produces is the
evidence the paper needs that the taxonomy is real rather than an
artefact of where a threshold was put.

THE FOUR LABELS
---------------
  L = LOCKED-IN     One wrong answer, fixed early, never seriously
                    challenged. The right answer never appears. Reads
                    as confident and wrong.

  I = INTERLEAVING  The RIGHT answer appears at some round, then the
                    model moves away from it and commits to a wrong one.
                    Marked <-- GOLD where it was spotted, but check by
                    eye: a common word like 'yes' may match by accident.

  C = INCONSISTENT  Several unrelated guesses, none of them held. Often
                    decays into word salad near the end.

  X = NEITHER       Truncated, an echo of the question, or a near-miss
                    that is arguably correct (e.g. 'Las Vegas' scored
                    against gold 'Clark County'). Say which in the note.

Trust your eye over the numbers. If a trace looks locked-in, write L,
whatever the statistics next to it say. Disagreement between your labels
and the code is the POINT of this exercise - it is what moves the
thresholds.
