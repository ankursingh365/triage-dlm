  HOW TO LABEL

  For each item, decide ONE thing: does the model answer state
  the same fact as one of the reference answers?

      y   yes, it is correct
      n   no, it is wrong
      ?   the reference answer itself looks broken, or the
          question cannot be answered as asked

  Ignore wording, word order, extra explanation, capitals and
  punctuation. An answer that only repeats the question, or
  never reaches an answer, is n.

  Send back one line of 100 labels, in order, separated by
  spaces. Example:  y n n y ? n y y n ...

  The judge's verdicts are NOT in this file, deliberately.
  Do not open step13a_judge_verdicts.csv before labelling -
  an annotator who has seen the machine's answer is not an
  independent annotator.
